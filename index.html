Instructions

Welcome to Solana Blackjack!

  1. Connect your wallet or generate a local keypair.
  2. Request an airdrop if needed.
  3. Use the PDA account balance options to manage funds.
  4. Place bets, hit, or stand to play the game.
  5. Admin options are for debugging but you can add funds to the treasury account if it's empty.

Enjoy the game!
